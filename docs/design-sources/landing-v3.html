🛡️ Poket SafeLine

Every student deserves
to feel safe at school.

Schools have duty-of-care obligations. Parents want to know their child is safe. Students need somewhere to turn. Poket SafeLine closes the gap — built directly into the platform, always on, always anonymous.

🧒
For students

Many students are afraid to speak up — afraid of being labelled a troublemaker, afraid of retaliation, or simply not knowing who to tell. SafeLine removes every barrier.

  • ✓Report anonymously — no name required
  • ✓Available 24/7 — not just during school hours
  • ✓Report bullying, harassment, threats, unsafe situations
  • ✓Report a concern about another student
  • ✓No judgement. No consequence for reporting.
👨‍👩‍👧
For parents

Parents often find out about bullying weeks or months after it started — or never at all. SafeLine gives parents a direct line into the institution when their child feels unable to speak.

  • ✓Parents can raise a concern on behalf of their child
  • ✓Receive alerts when a report involves your child
  • ✓Know that there is a system in place — not just a suggestion box
  • ✓See that the school takes welfare seriously
👩‍🏫
For teachers

Teachers cannot be everywhere. Many incidents happen outside school hours, online, or in spaces teachers cannot see. SafeLine extends the teacher's reach without adding to their workload.

  • ✓Alerts delivered to the right staff member automatically
  • ✓Clear audit trail — every report is logged and timestamped
  • ✓No guesswork — teachers know what was reported and when
  • ✓Supports duty-of-care documentation
🏫
For schools

Schools have a legal and moral duty of care. When an incident goes unreported and something happens, the institution is exposed. SafeLine gives schools a system they can point to — and a record to rely on.

  • ✓Built-in duty-of-care infrastructure
  • ✓Full audit log — who reported, when, what action was taken
  • ✓Designate which staff receive which alert types
  • ✓Demonstrate to parents and regulators that welfare is taken seriously
  • ✓No additional software or integration required
What can be reported
•Bullying — verbal, physical, or online
•Cyberbullying and online harassment
•Threats or intimidation
•Discrimination or exclusion
•A student who seems distressed or at risk
•An unsafe situation on or off campus
•Concern about a peer's wellbeing

FAQ

Common questions.

Is this a replacement for school?
No. Poket School is an AI intelligence layer that sits alongside schools, universities and institutions. We supplement — we do not replace. Students still attend their school or university. ET AI is their personal subject specialist available after hours, on weekends, and whenever they need help.
Which exam boards does it cover?
Poket School is exam-board neutral. Content is aligned with Cambridge International (CAIE), Pearson Edexcel, Oxford AQA, and the University of London LLB. We do not name exam boards inside the platform — we teach the subject at the level, so students are prepared for any board sitting.
Can a student switch programmes or subjects?
Yes. Students can change their enrolled programme or add subjects at any time from their settings. An IGCSE student who decides to add a language at CEFR level can do so on the same account.
What languages does the platform support?
The platform is available in 53 languages. ET AI teachers can explain and give feedback in the student's own language. Five right-to-left languages are fully supported including Arabic, which flips the entire page layout. Read-aloud in every language is coming soon.
How does the free student ID card work?
Every registered student receives a free digital ID card showing their name, programme, level, and a verified QR code. It is issued automatically on account creation — no application needed. It can be shown at libraries and partner institutions.
Is there a free trial?
Yes. The Free Preview tier lets any student browse the full course catalogue, take the first lesson of any course, and verify any Poket School certificate — all without a credit card. Upgrade to a paid plan when you are ready.
